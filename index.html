A few reasonable questions

Yes, you could roll your own.

Why pay if I can build this myself?

You can. The question is whether agent orchestration is the product you want to maintain. cofounder packages the roles, context, work queue, review steps, and recovery into a workflow you can use for your own product.

What does “$39 + tokens” mean?

The hosted platform is $39 per month. Model usage is a separate cost, so $39 is not an all-inclusive price or a promise of unlimited AI work. The model billing setup and usage controls will be confirmed during early-access onboarding, before you subscribe.

How autonomous is it?

The goal is minimal supervision within the direction you set. Progress depends on evidence such as tests and independent review. Changes to product goals, destructive operations, and expanded permissions require your decision.

Can I use it for an existing project?

That is a core use case, alongside starting from scratch. The initial focus is Ruby on Rails for web products and Expo for iOS. Repository compatibility and release integrations are checked during onboarding.

Is it ready today?

cofounder is in early access. The local development workflow is implemented; full production-release and iOS delivery paths are still being validated. Join the list to discuss your project and what is supported before committing.

What about open source and self-hosting?

cofounder is being built as an open-source product with a self-hosted option. The $39/month offer is for managed hosting. If running the infrastructure yourself is the right trade-off, self-hosting is part of the product’s direction.
