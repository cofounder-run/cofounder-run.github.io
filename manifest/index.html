The cofounder manifest

Build your company.

The AI-native operating system for your company.

A company is the people you serve, the decisions you make, the knowledge you build, and the work you do every day. Its product is one part of that whole.

You bring the ambition, direction, and judgment. We’re building cofounder so people and AI can work together across the company, from the first idea to daily operations.

One company. A shared context.

Research should inform product decisions. Customer conversations should inform growth. Results should inform what happens next. Keep the company’s knowledge, decisions, and work connected, so every task starts with what you already know.

Start with what the company needs.

Explore a market. Build a product. Find customers. Improve an operation. Begin with the outcome, choose the work it calls for, and follow through. A research question needs sources and a useful answer; a software change needs working software.

Setup is our job.

You shouldn’t spend your weekend configuring agents, connecting tools, and assembling a harness. We take responsibility for making the parts work together. Bring your AI subscription, connect your sources, and work with your cofounder from your browser or phone.

The workflow is the product.

Strong models can produce code, research, and plans. What matters is choosing the right work, carrying context between tasks, and checking the result. We choose thoughtful defaults and integrated tools. When the evidence points to a better approach, we change our minds.

“Done” needs proof.

For research, show sources and dates. For product work, demonstrate the behavior and relevant checks. For growth, distinguish a proposed experiment from a measured result. If something failed or remains uncertain, say so. Remember the lesson next time.

Make your attention count.

You set priorities, decide what matters, and choose when to change course. AI handles work within the boundaries you agree on and brings back decisions that need your judgment. Readable text, room to breathe, and a clear next step make that work a pleasure.

Your company should remain yours.

Your code, knowledge, sources, decisions, and history should remain yours to inspect, change, and take with you. Invite your team and build a shared understanding. Good defaults help you get started. You keep the final say.

Our compass: the care for craft, integrated workflows, and human attention found in DHH, Jakob Nielsen, and the other heroes of the old internet. Their philosophy, our interpretation. Choose carefully, make the parts work together, and care about every detail.

Ideas we build on

Ideas that inform how we build cofounder:

  • DHH’s Rails Doctrine and Omarchy: thoughtful defaults, integrated tools, craft, and ownership.
  • Jakob Nielsen’s heuristics: show the system’s state, speak the user’s language, prevent errors, and keep every screen minimal.
  • spec-kit: constitution, clarify before planning, separate WHAT from HOW.
  • superpowers: two-stage review, evidence before claims, TDD iron law.
  • gstack: adapted quote-the-evidence verification and file-anchored learnings.
  • OpenSpec: numbered WHEN/THEN scenarios as the TDD coverage contract.
  • Minto’s The Pyramid Principle: MECE proposals and scenarios, without overlaps or gaps.
  • Adversarial-critics TDD: refute coverage claims and check that tests catch deliberate mutations.
  • hyper-code: persistent agent workspaces, tools, and artifacts together.

We’re in early development. This is the standard we’re building toward.
