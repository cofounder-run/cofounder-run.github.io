# Product direction

Read `docs/product-compass.md` before making product, design, architecture, or engineering decisions. The agreed lens for every decision is **“the harness DHH would build”**, informed by Omarchy: thoughtful defaults, integrated workflows, craft, ownership, and evidence-based choice of tools.

Apply the lens proportionately. Briefly explain substantive tradeoffs in the existing plan, PR, or decision record. Routine reversible work needs no extra approval or standalone document. Treat the phrase as our product interpretation, not a claim about DHH’s personal approval or a mandate for a language.
